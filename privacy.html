Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 August 2026

This policy describes the privacy posture of the public BULLSEYE product site and the internal League of Legends analytics product it represents.

Public website

This public informational site does not provide user accounts, forms, advertising, behavioral profiling, or direct user tracking functionality. It exists to describe BULLSEYE, publish product policies, and provide product-verification files required by platform providers.

League of Legends data

BULLSEYE may process League of Legends identifiers and game data obtained through authorized sources, including Riot Games APIs and separately authorized professional-data providers. Depending on the workflow, this may include Riot IDs, PUUIDs, summoner/account metadata, ranked information, match history, match timelines, champion selections, builds, runes and other game-related records made available by the relevant authorized service.

Purpose of processing

Game data is processed for post-match analytics, competitive intelligence, player and team review, champion-pool analysis, ranked context, VOD review, statistical comparison and related coaching decision-support workflows.

Professional and private data

Authorized professional competition and team-private data, including scrim information where available to the organization, are governed separately from public/ranked Riot API data. Private scrim data is confidential and is not intentionally exposed through this public site.

Data sharing and resale

BULLSEYE is not designed as a raw-data resale or pass-through service. We do not intentionally sell access to raw Riot API payloads or redistribute confidential team data through this public site. Data handling remains subject to the applicable provider agreements and platform policies.

Retention and security

Operational data is retained only as necessary for the relevant analytical workflows and product operation, subject to provider rules and internal data-governance requirements. BULLSEYE uses access controls and structural separation between public, competitive and private team-data domains.

Riot account sign-in

The current internal version of BULLSEYE does not require public Riot Sign On (RSO) for users. If a future product flow introduces Riot-account authorization, the registered product and this policy will be updated before that functionality is released.

Changes

This policy may be updated as BULLSEYE evolves, its data sources change, or additional product functionality is introduced.

Contact

Privacy inquiries may be directed to the product owner using the contact information associated with the registered BULLSEYE application in the Riot Developer Portal.
