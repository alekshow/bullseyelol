Purpose

BULLSEYE is a competitive-intelligence, post-match analytics and coaching decision-support product. It is designed to organize authorized League of Legends data into evidence, historical statistics, review queues and analytical context for professional esports operations.

No real-time gameplay assistance

BULLSEYE is not intended to provide real-time in-game instructions, automate gameplay, reveal unauthorized hidden information or remove gameplay decisions from players. Analytical outputs are intended for review, preparation and post-match use.
